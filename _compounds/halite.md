---
title: Halite
formula: NaCl
found: >-
  Rock salt: thick beds left behind where ancient seas dried up, such as the Permian basins under
  Cheshire, northern Germany and West Texas, and the salt domes of the Gulf Coast, where buried salt
  has flowed upward through the rock above it. It also crusts modern salt flats and lakes like the
  Bonneville Salt Flats and the Dead Sea, and seawater is about 3.5% dissolved salt, most of it this.
mined: >-
  Underground by room-and-pillar mining with continuous miners, as at Goderich in Ontario, the largest
  salt mine in the world, and Winsford in Cheshire; by solution mining, where water is pumped down a
  well to dissolve the salt and the brine is boiled down in vacuum pans; and by evaporating seawater
  or lake brine in shallow ponds under the sun. Most rock salt goes onto icy roads and into the
  chemical industry, which splits it into chlorine and sodium hydroxide.
---
Salt was mined at Wieliczka near Kraków from the thirteenth century until 1996, and the miners carved
chapels, statues and chandeliers out of the salt as they went. The structure is the simplest one a
crystal can have: sodium and chlorine alternate like the squares of a three-dimensional chessboard,
every ion surrounded by six of the other kind. Galena has the same arrangement.
