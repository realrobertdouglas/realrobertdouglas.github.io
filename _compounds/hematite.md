---
title: Hematite
formula: Fe2O3
found: >-
  The most important iron ore. The largest deposits are banded iron formations, such as the Pilbara in
  Western Australia, Carajás in Brazil and Kryvyi Rih in Ukraine. It also colors many soils and
  sedimentary rocks red.
mined: >-
  Mostly open pit: the ore is drilled, blasted and loaded into haul trucks. High-grade ore is crushed,
  screened and shipped as lump and fines. Lower-grade ore is concentrated by gravity or magnetic
  separation and flotation, then rolled into pellets for blast furnaces.
