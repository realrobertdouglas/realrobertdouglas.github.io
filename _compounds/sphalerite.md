---
title: Sphalerite
formula: ZnS
found: >-
  The ore of zinc, nearly always together with galena, in hydrothermal veins and in beds of limestone
  where metal-rich brines came through: Red Dog in Alaska, the largest zinc mine in the world, Broken
  Hill in Australia, Rammelsberg in Germany and the Tri-State district around Joplin, Missouri. Pure
  crystals are honey-colored; iron in the lattice turns most of it black.
mined: >-
  Open pit at Red Dog and underground elsewhere. The ore is crushed and ground, and selective froth
  flotation lifts the sphalerite from the galena and the waste into a concentrate of about 50% zinc.
  The concentrate is roasted to zinc oxide, dissolved in sulfuric acid and plated out as zinc metal by
  electrolysis. Cadmium, germanium and indium come out of it as by-products. Half of all zinc goes to
  galvanizing steel.
---
The name is from the Greek for treacherous: miners took it for galena and got no lead. The structure
is diamond's with every other atom swapped for the other element, and the semiconductors gallium
arsenide and indium phosphide take the same form.
