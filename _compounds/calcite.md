---
title: Calcite
formula: CaCO3
found: >-
  The main mineral in limestone, chalk and marble, and in cave formations like stalactites. Clear
  crystals, called Iceland spar, come from Helgustaðir in Iceland.
mined: >-
  Limestone and marble are quarried in open pits by drilling and blasting, or cut into blocks with wire
  saws for building stone. The rock is crushed and screened for aggregate, ground fine for fillers, or
  burned in kilns to make lime and cement.
