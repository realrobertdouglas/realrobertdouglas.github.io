---
title: Chalcopyrite
formula: CuFeS2
found: >-
  The most common copper mineral. Most of it comes from porphyry copper deposits such as Escondida and
  Chuquicamata in Chile, Bingham Canyon in Utah and Grasberg in Indonesia. It also occurs in massive
  sulfide deposits on old sea floors and in skarns.
mined: >-
  Mostly open pit, with some underground block caving. The ore, often under 1% copper, is crushed and
  ground fine, and froth flotation lifts the chalcopyrite into a concentrate of 25 to 30% copper. Smelting
  turns the concentrate into matte and then blister copper, which is refined by electrolysis.
