---
title: Quartz
formula: SiO2
found: >-
  The most common mineral at the Earth's surface after the feldspars: a main ingredient of granite,
  gneiss and sandstone, the grains of most beaches and dunes, and the veins that carry gold. Clear
  crystals come from Minas Gerais in Brazil, the Ouachita Mountains of Arkansas and the Swiss Alps.
  Amethyst, citrine, smoky quartz, rose quartz, agate and flint are all quartz.
mined: >-
  Mostly as silica sand, dug or dredged from sand deposits and sandstone quarries, then washed and
  screened for glassmaking, foundry molds and fracking proppant. High-purity quartz for the crucibles
  that silicon ingots are grown in comes from pegmatites at Spruce Pine, North Carolina, crushed and
  cleaned by flotation and acid leaching. The flawless crystals in watches and radios are not mined
  at all but grown in autoclaves from dissolved quartz.
---
Quartz is piezoelectric: squeeze a crystal and a voltage appears across it, apply a voltage and it
flexes. A sliver cut to vibrate 32,768 times a second is the heart of every quartz clock. In the
structure every silicon sits in a tetrahedron of four oxygens, and the tetrahedra share corners in
helices that wind the same way through the whole crystal, so a crystal is either left- or
right-handed.
