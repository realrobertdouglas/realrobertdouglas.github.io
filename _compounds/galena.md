---
title: Galena
formula: PbS
found: >-
  Hydrothermal veins and replacement deposits in limestone, often together with sphalerite. Well-known
  sources include the Viburnum Trend in Missouri, Broken Hill in Australia and Freiberg in Germany.
  It often carries silver.
mined: >-
  Underground by room-and-pillar mining or stoping, and in some places open pit. The ore is crushed and
  ground, and selective froth flotation separates galena from sphalerite and waste rock. Its high density
  (7.6 g/cm³) also suits gravity separation. The concentrate goes to a smelter.
