---
title: Methane
formula: CH4
isomers:
  - name: Methane
    structure: CH4
    atoms: [[C, 0, 0], [H, 0, -1], [H, 1, 0], [H, 0, 1], [H, -1, 0]]
    bonds: [[1, 2], [1, 3], [1, 4], [1, 5]]
    made: >-
      The main part of natural gas, recovered from gas wells and oil fields and dried and cleaned before it enters pipelines.
      Bacteria make it when organic matter breaks down without air, which is how biogas and landfill gas are produced.
      It can also be made from carbon dioxide and hydrogen over a nickel catalyst (the Sabatier reaction).
