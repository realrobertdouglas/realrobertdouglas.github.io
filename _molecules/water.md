    made: >-
      Forms whenever hydrogen burns in oxygen, and whenever a fuel that contains hydrogen burns.
      It is also a product of acid and base neutralization and of respiration.
      The water we use is not made but collected and cleaned by filtration, distillation or reverse osmosis.
