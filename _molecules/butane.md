---
title: Butane
formula: C4H10
isomers:
  - name: n-Butane
    structure: CH3CH2CH2CH3
    made: >-
      Separated from natural gas liquids and from refinery gases by fractional distillation.
      It is rarely made on purpose, since both sources carry plenty of it.
  - name: Isobutane
    structure: (CH3)3CH
    made: >-
      Separated from natural gas liquids and refinery streams by distillation, and made from n-butane
      by isomerization over a platinum catalyst on chlorided alumina. Refineries use it in alkylation units
      to make high-octane gasoline blendstock.
---
