---
title: Carbon dioxide
formula: CO2
isomers:
  - name: Carbon dioxide
    structure: O=C=O
    atoms: [[O, -1, 0], [C, 0, 0], [O, 1, 0]]
    bonds: [[1, 2, 2], [2, 3, 2]]
    made: >-
      Released by burning carbon fuels, by respiration and by fermentation.
      Industrial carbon dioxide is mostly captured as a by-product of hydrogen and ammonia plants
      and of ethanol fermentation, or drawn from natural carbon dioxide wells.
      Heating limestone (calcite) to make lime also releases it.
